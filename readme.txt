---

## 💡 Solution

> We built an AI-powered Energy Optimizer that forecasts demand spikes, detects anomalies in renewable-energy 
assets, identifies their likely root causes, and recommends load-balancing actions. It helps energy operators 
make faster, data-driven decisions, reduce unnecessary renewable-energy curtailment, and improve overall grid 
efficiency.
---

## ✨ Key Features

Feature 1: AI-powered demand forecasting to predict upcoming electricity demand spikes and consumption patterns.
Feature 2: Intelligent load balancing that recommends actions to maintain grid stability and optimize energy distribution.
Feature 3: Real-time anomaly detection to identify unusual performance in renewable-energy assets.
Feature 4: Root cause analysis to identify why an asset is underperforming and prioritize corrective actions.
Feature 5: AI-generated operator briefs with actionable recommendations and a curtailment-minimization plan.

## ⚠️ Known Limitations
    1:-Operator Recommendations:AI recommendations are decision-support suggestions and still require 
                        human verification before being applied to an actual power grid.
    2:-Limited Asset Integration: The prototype is not yet connected directly to SCADA, smart meters, 
                        IoT sensors, or real renewable-energy equipment.

## 🏅 What We're Most Proud Of
    It connects forecasting, anomaly detection, root-cause analysis, load balancing, and operator 
    recommendations into one workflow.he key value is turning complex energy data into clear, actionable 
    decisions that can help reduce renewable-energy curtailment and improve grid efficiency.
